Main Content

Since October 7, Gaza has suffered catastrophic losses. Thousands of civilians, including women and children, have been killed or injured in the ongoing aggression. Entire neighborhoods were destroyed, and vital infrastructure such as hospitals, schools, and water systems have been severely damaged.

According to reports from Sehatty and Al Jazeera, more than 40,000 people have been martyred since the start of the war, including over 15,000 children and 10,000 women. Over80% of Gaza’s population has been displaced, and basic services such as food, water, and healthcare have collapsed due to continuous bombardments.

Many residential areas have been completely leveled, leaving families without shelter. Hospitals are overwhelmed with the injured and lack essential medical equipment. Entire communities are living in tents, struggling to find clean water, food, and electricity as humanitarian aid remains severely restricted.
