Key Information

  • Capital: Al-Quds
  • Area: 27,027 km²
  • Official Language: Arabic
  • Religion: Islam (predominant) & Christianity
  • Main cities: Al-Quds, Gaza, Ramallah, Nablus

Brief History

The State of Palestine has a long and complex history that dates back thousands of years. It is a region of great cultural and religious significance to many peoples.

Throughout history, Palestine has been ruled by various empires and civilizations, including the Romans, Ottomans, and British. The modern political situation remains one of the most debated in the world.

Despite challenges, the Palestinian people maintain a strong sense of identity and continue to strive for peace and independence.
